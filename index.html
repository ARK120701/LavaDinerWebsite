A neighborhood counter that runs hot

Lava Diner opened on Hillside Ave to do one thing well: cook diner food properly, all day, for whoever walks through the door. That means eggs cracked to order, burgers pressed on a real flat-top, and coffee that never sits too long.

Ask around and regulars will tell you their order before they've even sat down — that's the kind of counter this is.
